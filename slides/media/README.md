# Presentation media

Both MP4 files are embedded in the PPTX. PDF posters and labeled footer buttons
link to downloadable release copies. Test playback on the presentation computer;
PDF viewers show posters and links rather than embedded video playback.

- `purt-fpv-chase.mp4`: supplied September 2025 PURT FPV chase recording,
  also included in the supplied PURT presentation. Compressed from the original
  4K movie to 1080p/30 fps H.264 with AAC audio and MP4 fast-start metadata.
  Show a short excerpt; the full recording is about six minutes.
- `fastdyn-monte-carlo.mp4`: supplied public demonstration recording, retained
  at its original 1600×900 resolution and approximately 30-second duration.
  The poster is the supplied Monte Carlo image. Its 200 recorded flights / 100
  paired wind scenarios are separate from the workshop's runnable three-load
  example. See the main slide's notes and [FastDyn guide](../../docs/FASTDYN.md).
